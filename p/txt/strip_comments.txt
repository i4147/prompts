Strip comments from source files recursively.
Prompt: Write a Python CLI that recursively strips comments from Rust, TOML, JavaScript/TypeScript,
Python, Shell, and Lua files.
Use multiprocessing.Pool with a fixed pool of 8 workers via apply_async.
Parse source with hand-written lexers that respect strings, raw strings, chars,
and nested block comments for Rust; string-aware hash comments for TOML/Shell;
template literals and block comments for JS/TS;
tokenize-based preservation of shebangs, type/fmt/noqa pragmas,
and module/nested docstrings for Python; long-bracket strings and comments for Lua.
Collapse runs of blank lines to at most two.
Report per-file results and a summary with line and byte savings using loguru and ANSI colors.
