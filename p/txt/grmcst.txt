Strip comments and docstrings from Python source files using libcst.
Preserves shebang lines, `# type:` directives,
and `# fmt:` pragmas while removing all other comments and module/function/class docstrings.
Discovers targets from CLI path arguments (defaults to CWD),
processes them in a fixed multiprocessing.Pool of 8 workers, validates the result with ast.parse,
and reports per-file status via loguru.
