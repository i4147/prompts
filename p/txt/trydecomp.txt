Universal archive / compression detector, decompressor, and extractor.
Pipeline: STAGE 1 Magic-byte sniff (fast hint — no decoding) STAGE 2 libarchive catch-all
(covers ~40 archive formats) STAGE 3 Individual decompressors (fallback for raw streams) Extraction:
Any format that is successfully recognized in any stage is registered,
and if at least one was recognized the input is extracted to the CWD with zip-slip / tar-slip
protection.
Usage: python this_script.py <filename>
