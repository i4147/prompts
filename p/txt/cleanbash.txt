Remove comments from bash/shell scripts in place,
using tree-sitter for accurate parsing
(so '#' inside strings, parameter expansions, etc. is never mistaken for a comment).
After stripping comments, runs of 2+ consecutive blank lines are collapsed to a single blank line,
and the resulting source is re-parsed
and checked for syntax errors before it is written back to disk.
Files that would become invalid are left untouched and reported as errors.
