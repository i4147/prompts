This is invoked in a subprocess to call the build backend hooks.
It expects: - Command line args: hook_name, control_dir - Environment variables:
_PYPROJECT_HOOKS_BUILD_BACKEND=entry.point:spec _PYPROJECT_HOOKS_BACKEND_PATH=paths
(separated with os.pathsep) - control_dir/input.json: - {"kwargs": {...}} Results:
- control_dir/output.json - {"return_val": ...}
