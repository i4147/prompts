Usage: download [--all | --annotators NAME] Options:
--annotators STR Annotators' resources to download.
You can list the names in a comma-separated string.
Supported annotators include:
deid:philter secsplit:regex secsplit:medspacy ssplit:nltk ner:regex ner:spacy parse:bllip tree2dep
neg:negbio --bllip-model DIR [default: ~/.radtext/bllipparser] --spacy-model NAME
[default: en_core_web_sm]
