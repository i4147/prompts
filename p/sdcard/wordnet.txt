An NLTK interface for WordNet WordNet is a lexical database of English.
Using synsets, helps find conceptual relationships between words such as hypernyms, hyponyms,
synonyms, antonyms etc.
For details about WordNet see:
https://wordnet.princeton.edu/ This module also allows you to find lemmas in languages other than
English from the Open Multilingual Wordnet https://omwn.org/
