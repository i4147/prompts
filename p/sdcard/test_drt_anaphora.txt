Regression tests for the quadratic time+memory DoS in DRT anaphora resolution
(CWE-770; CVE-2026-12873). ``nltk.sem.drt.resolve_anaphora`` resolves each pronoun condition by
scanning every discourse referent on the trail
and retaining the compatible ones as candidate antecedents.
A DRS with N referents and N ``PRO`` conditions therefore costs O(N**2) time and retained memory,
so a small crafted DRS string exhausts the process.
The total number of (pronoun, referent) examinations is now bounded by ``MAX_ANAPHORA_OPERATIONS``;
once exceeded, resolution raises ``AnaphoraResolutionException``.
